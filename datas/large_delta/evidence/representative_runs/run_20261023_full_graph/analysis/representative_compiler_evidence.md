# Representative large-|Δ| compiler evidence

This report compares the four targeted configurations for the selected cases. In full-graph captures, experimental RUSTFLAGS apply to the benchmark crate and its Cargo dependencies, matching the scope of the historical runner; LLVM and assembly counters aggregate all emitted dependency modules. Primary-crate-only captures are labelled separately. MIR and LLVM structure counts are descriptive, do not identify a hot path, and do not prove a causal mechanism. `llvm_optimization_remarks_by_cell.csv` summarizes the pass, remark-name, function, and local source-location evidence extracted from the raw remark files; the complete YAML remains preserved under each cell. Remarks identify compiler-reported optimization events, but do not establish that an event caused the runtime interaction. Compiler artifacts were collected with the supplemental toolchain/source snapshot recorded in `compiler_environment.json`, which does not match the historical experiment environment. They are therefore supporting evidence only and are not merged into the historical runtime estimates.
